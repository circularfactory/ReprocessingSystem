Introduction back to ToC

In a circular factory, used products are disassembled and their components are inspected, reprocessed and reused in new product generations. Each returning component has an individual history and damage pattern, so reprocessing cannot rely on fixed process plans. It requires that the information about a component, the process applied to it and the resulting quality is available in a machine-interpretable form across the different stations of the factory.

Subproject C02 of the Collaborative Research Centre SFB 1574 develops a reprocessing cell in which damaged regions of metallic components are removed by machining, rebuilt by high-speed directed energy deposition with laser beam and metal powder (HS DED-LB/M) and finish-machined. The deposition process is observed by several measurement systems, such as optical coherence tomography, two-colour pyrometry, high-speed imaging, emission spectroscopy, acoustic emission and powder caustic analysis, and data-driven process models estimate characteristics of the deposited material from process parameters and monitoring features.

The Reprocessing System Ontology provides the vocabulary to describe this cell in the knowledge graph of the circular factory. It specialises the Circular Factory Core Ontology for resources, capabilities, processes, tasks and operations, and reuses established ontologies wherever possible: SOSA for sensors, actuations and observations, QUDT for quantity values, units and uncertainty, and PROV-O for the provenance of datasets and model predictions. An optional module aligns the ontology with DOLCE+DnS Ultralite.

The ontology is designed so that it answers questions such as: Which process parameters were applied in a given deposition run? Which observations, with which uncertainty, were made on a deposited layer, and from which raw dataset were they computed? Which model predicted a deposit characteristic, and on which inputs was the prediction based? Which reprocessing operations has a component undergone?

Version 0.1.0 is a first release. Known open issues, in particular the alignment of the uncertainty representation with other subprojects and pending changes of the Core Ontology, are documented as editorial notes in the ontology and in the issue list of the repository.
