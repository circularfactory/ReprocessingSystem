Abstract

The Reprocessing System Ontology describes the semi-autonomous additive-subtractive reprocessing of used metallic components in the circular factory of the Collaborative Research Centre SFB 1574. It extends the Circular Factory Core Ontology with the resources of a reprocessing cell, the reprocessing process chain of damage removal by machining, material build-up by high-speed directed energy deposition with laser beam and metal powder (HS DED-LB/M) and finish machining, as well as the operations and process runs through which this chain is executed. Process parameter settings, in-process observations and predictions of data-driven process models are represented with the W3C SOSA ontology, quantity values and their measurement or prediction uncertainty with QUDT, and the provenance of monitoring datasets with PROV-O. The ontology thereby links each reprocessed component to the parameters, observations and data recorded during its reprocessing, and provides the basis for representing causal knowledge between process settings and the resulting component state in future versions.
